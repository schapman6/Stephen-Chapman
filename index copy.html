Research journey

From photosynthesis
to mammalian metabolism

Across plants and animals, my research asks how interactions between biological processes give rise to the behaviour of a whole system.

Manchester

PhD · Systems Biology

Photosynthesis & metabolic flexibility

My doctoral research combined mathematical modelling with experiments to investigate photosynthesis and cellular metabolism. Work on Chlamydomonas reinhardtii explored how acetate metabolism regulates photosynthesis and central carbon metabolism.

Read the metabolic modelling paper ↗

Aberystwyth

Teaching & scholarship

Bioinformatics, biotechnology & education

Teaching biotechnology and bioinformatics brought research and education together. My work has also included collaborative contributions on microalgae, sustainability and the circular economy, alongside an interest in creative approaches to teaching science.

Microalgae and the circular economy ↗

Aix-Marseille

Postdoctoral research

Mitochondria & cellular signalling

In France, I applied computational systems biology to mitochondrial metabolism, metabolic disease and cancer. This research includes MitoMAMMAL, a model of mammalian mitochondrial metabolism, and logical modelling of interactions in the myelofibrotic cellular microenvironment.

MitoMAMMAL ↗Logical modelling ↗

Liverpool

Computational biology

Research, supervision learning & widening the student experience

I now bring these perspectives into computational biology research, student supervision and teaching. My interests include metabolic modelling, omics integration, reproducible analysis and authentic assessment in an education landscape shaped by AI.

05 / About

A systems view of biology,
An active approach to learning

I’m a Lecturer in Computational Biology and Programme Director of Bioinformatics at the University of Liverpool.

My route into systems biology has taken me from a PhD at Manchester to teaching at Aberystwyth and postdoctoral research at Aix-Marseille. Across these settings, I’ve worked on questions spanning photosynthesis, mitochondrial metabolism and cellular signalling.

My teaching connects biological questions with practical computational investigation. Through modelling, programming and discussion, I help students develop the confidence to explore ideas, interpret evidence and critically evaluate their analyses.
